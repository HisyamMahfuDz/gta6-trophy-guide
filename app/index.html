    Road to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    The list is built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, then side content, then collectibles and activities, then the money and combat cleanup, with 100% completion last. Launch guides put the whole road at roughly 200 hours; the story itself is about 50 of them. There are no difficulty trophies and no online trophies, so the Platinum is long, not hard.

      How to read the roadmap

      Each stage lists the trophies it covers and the order to earn them in. Tick trophies off in the Tracker as you go — the progress bar at the top follows you. The only hard deadline in the whole list is in Stage 3: meditate at Lucia’s balcony spot before you finish Chapter 1.

      Missable trophies & save strategy

      Good news first: almost nothing in this list can be missed. The one exception is a single meditation spot, and the fix is a habit, not a replay: keep manual saves.

      Save strategy — no mission replay

      Launch guides report that GTA 6 has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. That makes a manual save your only way back. Keep three rolling saves and refresh them at these moments:

      1. At the start of every chapter (Chapters 1–4 and the finale).
      2. Before the final mission — so you can try a different ending choice or mop up anything left behind.
      3. Before any big story choice — choices feed the Actions Speak Louder counter.
